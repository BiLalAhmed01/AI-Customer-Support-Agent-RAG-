# Returns, Refunds, Cancellations, and Exchanges

## Return Policy

You may return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Items must be in original packaging with tags attached. To start
a return, log into your account, go to "Order History," and select
"Start a Return."

## Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the
returned item, and issued to your original payment method. You'll
receive an email confirmation once the refund is complete.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for a different color or model, start a standard
return and place a new order for the item you want — this is faster than
waiting for an exchange to process. Exchanges are not automatic.

## Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing them, by
contacting support immediately. After 1 hour, orders may have already
entered fulfillment and cannot be cancelled — you can return the item
once delivered instead.

## Final Sale Items

Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange.
This is clearly noted on the product page before purchase.
