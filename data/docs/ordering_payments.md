# Ordering and Payments

## How to Place an Order

Customers can purchase products through our online store at
shop.acmewidgets.example. Browse the catalog, add items to your cart, and
check out using any of our accepted payment methods. Guest checkout is
available — no account required.

## Pricing

The Widget Desk Lamp is $49.99, the Widget Hub is $89.99, and the Widget
Charging Station is $34.99. Prices are listed in USD and include
applicable taxes at checkout based on your shipping address.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not
currently support Apple Pay, Google Pay, or cryptocurrency payments.
Premium support plan billing occurs on the same calendar day each month
as your original signup date.

## Order Confirmation

Order confirmation emails are sent immediately after checkout. If you
don't see one within 15 minutes, check your spam folder or contact
support with your order number.

## Discounts and Promotions

We occasionally run promotional discounts announced via email newsletter.
Discount codes are entered at checkout and cannot be combined with other
offers or applied retroactively to past orders.
