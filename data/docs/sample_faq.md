# Acme Widgets — Support FAQ

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US.
Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
additional $12.99. International shipping takes 10-21 business days
depending on destination and customs processing.

## Returns and Refunds

You may return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be in original packaging with tags attached. To start a return,
log into your account, go to "Order History," and select "Start a Return."
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

Sale items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange.

## Account and Billing

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and
follow the emailed link. The link expires after 24 hours.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not
currently support Apple Pay or Google Pay. Subscription billing occurs
on the same calendar day each month as your original signup date.

## Product Warranty

All Acme Widgets products carry a 1-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse,
water exposure, or unauthorized repairs. To file a warranty claim,
contact support@acmewidgets.example with your order number and a
description of the issue.

## Contacting Support

Live chat is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST. Email support
(support@acmewidgets.example) typically responds within 1 business day.
Phone support is available for Premium plan subscribers only, at
1-800-555-0199.
