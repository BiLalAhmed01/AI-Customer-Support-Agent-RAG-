# Contact, Support Hours, and FAQ

## Contacting Support

Live chat is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST. Email support
(support@acmewidgets.example) typically responds within 1 business day.
Phone support is available for Premium plan subscribers only, at
1-800-555-0199.

## Business Hours

Our support team operates Monday through Friday, 9am-6pm EST, excluding
major US holidays. The online store itself is available 24/7 for
browsing and placing orders.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, to most countries, with delivery in 10-21 business days.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?
A: Contact support within 1 hour of ordering; after that the order may
already be in fulfillment.

Q: Do you offer price matching?
A: No, we do not currently offer price matching with other retailers.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Not currently — the online store is accessible through any mobile
browser at shop.acmewidgets.example.

Q: How do I unsubscribe from marketing emails?
A: Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email, or update
your preferences in Account Settings.
