## About Acme Widgets

Acme Widgets is a demo e-commerce brand founded in 2021, operating
exclusively online at shop.acmewidgets.example. We design and sell
premium desk accessories and smart-home gadgets for home offices.

## Our Products

Acme Widgets offers three product lines: the Widget Desk Lamp (adjustable
smart lighting), the Widget Hub (a smart-home control device), and the
Widget Charging Station (multi-device wireless charging pad).

## Product Materials and Compatibility

Widget products are built from recycled aluminum and BPA-free plastics.
Each device connects via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth and is compatible with iOS,
Android, and major smart-home platforms including Alexa and Google Home.

## Company Values

Acme Widgets is built around sustainable, minimalist product design. All
packaging is recyclable, and every purchase plants one tree through our
reforestation partner (demo content only, not a real program).
