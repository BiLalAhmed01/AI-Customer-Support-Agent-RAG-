# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping Options

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US.
Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
additional $12.99. International shipping takes 10-21 business days
depending on destination and customs processing.

## Delivery Times by Region

US continental: 5-7 business days standard. US Alaska/Hawaii: 7-10
business days. Canada: 8-14 business days. Europe and UK: 10-18 business
days. Rest of world: up to 21 business days.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Track
your package anytime by logging into your account and visiting "Order
History," or using the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.

## Shipping Costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a
flat $5.99 standard shipping fee. Expedited and international shipping
rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

If your tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, contact
support with your order number and we'll investigate with the carrier.
