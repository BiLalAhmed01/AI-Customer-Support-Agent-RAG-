# Account Management and Warranty

## Managing Your Account

Create an account at checkout or anytime at shop.acmewidgets.example to
track order history, save shipping addresses, and manage payment methods.
An account is optional — guest checkout is always available.

## Resetting Your Password

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and
follow the emailed link. The link expires after 24 hours. If it expires,
simply request a new one.

## Updating Account Information

Update your email, shipping addresses, or saved payment methods anytime
from the "Account Settings" page after logging in. Changes take effect
immediately.

## Product Warranty

All Acme Widgets products carry a 1-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse,
water exposure, or unauthorized repairs.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

To file a warranty claim, contact support@acmewidgets.example with your
order number and a description of the issue. Our team typically responds
to warranty claims within 2 business days.
